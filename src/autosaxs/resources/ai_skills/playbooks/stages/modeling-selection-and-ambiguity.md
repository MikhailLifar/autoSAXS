# Stage: Modeling selection & ambiguity

### MD-SEL-1 — Choose model family by contrast & dispersity

- **Regime:** After Guinier/Kratky/IFT triage.
- **Observable signatures:** Uniform dilute bio → DAMMIF/`model-dam` + AMBIMETER/SASRES (Franke 2017; Tuukkanen 2016). Inhomogeneous contrast → DENSS/`model-density` (Grant 2018). Poly spheres → McSAS/`model-dr-mc` + mixture. Discrete oligomers → `calc-profile`+`model-lc`.
- **Physical cause vs artifact twin:** Over-interpreting bead models on mixtures (Jacques 2010).
- **Action in autosaxs:** Route via sample playbook; metrics advisory.
- **When to proceed despite weak metrics:** Ambiguous reconstructions still publishable with AMBIMETER/SASRES reported.
- **Primary citation:** Franke 2017; Tuukkanen 2016; Grant 2018; Jacques 2010; Manalastas-Cantos 2021
- **Trust:** Tier A/B
